Ishan Kashyap is a Civil Engineering graduate and entrepreneur based in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India.

He is a Director of Runya Ventures Private Limited and the Proprietor of Nirmal Industries, with professional involvement in manufacturing, furniture production, government procurement, business development and day-to-day business operations.

His professional interests include manufacturing, infrastructure, business management, government supply and entrepreneurship.

With a background in civil engineering and practical experience in business operations, Ishan works across technical, commercial and management functions , with a focus on developing and expanding manufacturing and supply businesses in Chhattisgarh.
